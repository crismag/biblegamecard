## Catalogue governance

- A human must confirm this target list and add any approved card families; directory presence is not catalogue authority.
- All statuses begin at `planned` because draft packages and reported prototype history do not satisfy evidence gates in the production status model.
- Title, archetype, traits, and provisional ability data remain in `registry/legendary_cards.json`; this report does not duplicate them.
- Collector-ID conflicts must be resolved without silently renumbering historical files.
