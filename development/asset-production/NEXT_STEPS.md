# Next Steps

## Work package 01 — Art Bible and Card Visual Specification

This is the next active milestone. Consolidate the existing guides under
`knowledge/art/` and review standards under `docs/` into an authoritative,
reviewable visual system. Do not create new platform code.

Required outputs:

- `ART_BIBLE.md` covering audience, tone, rendering, historical grounding,
  composition, lighting, colour, prohibited patterns, and uncertainty handling;
- `CARD_VISUAL_SPECIFICATION.md` covering hierarchy, frame, typography,
  dimensions, bleed/safe regions, icons, rarity, numbering, and card backs;
- `IMAGE_REVIEW_CRITERIA.md` defining human review gates and evidence.

Human decisions required before completion include target audience, rendering
style, historical-reference policy, rarity treatment, card dimensions, fonts,
and licensed reference/brand assets.
