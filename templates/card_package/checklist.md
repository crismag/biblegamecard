# Card Review Checklist

## Identity
- [ ] Collector ID matches registry
- [ ] Name, title, set, and status are consistent
- [ ] Defining contribution is specific and distinct

## Biblical and theological review
- [ ] Primary passages are listed
- [ ] Explicit facts, inferences, traditions, and creative choices are distinguished
- [ ] Ability and artwork do not contradict Scripture
- [ ] Miracles remain acts of God
- [ ] Disputed interpretations are documented

## Gameplay
- [ ] Strategic role is distinct
- [ ] Ability belongs specifically to this character
- [ ] Rules concept is testable
- [ ] Synergy, counterplay, and risks are recorded

## Artwork
- [ ] Canonical appearance is followed
- [ ] Scene and symbols are traceable
- [ ] Composition preserves text-safe zones
- [ ] No unapproved anachronism or symbolism
- [ ] Silhouette and focal hierarchy work at card size

## Generation and release
- [ ] Prompt sources are versioned
- [ ] Generated artifact metadata is recorded
- [ ] Card text spelling and references are verified
- [ ] Layout and export meet technical standards
- [ ] Required reviewers approved the artifact
