## Trait design rules

- Traits must represent biblically grounded strengths, not generic fantasy magic.
- Elemental language may be used where Scripture itself supplies the imagery, such as Elijah and fire.
- No character should be desirable only because of fame; each must possess a distinct gameplay advantage.
- Similar characters must differ mechanically. For example, Moses controls and liberates; Joshua conquers; Caleb claims fortified territory.
- Titles should communicate the person’s defining biblical legacy while retaining the forceful delivery tone of a premium fighting or strategy card.
