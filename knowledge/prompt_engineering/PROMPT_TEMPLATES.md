# Prompt Templates

## Purpose
Defines the minimum prompt fields for major asset families. Templates select relevant context; they do not replace reviewed source specifications.

## Legendary character
Required: character identity, life stage, narrative beat, expression, pose, garments, signature object, environment, composition, lighting, continuity references, style modules, quality modules, exclusions, aspect ratio.

## Character portrait
Required: facial continuity, age, hair, beard if applicable, expression, upper garment, simple contextual background, portrait crop, face-safe overlay zones, clean-art exclusions.

## Event scene
Required: event, scriptural scope, principal figures, one decisive moment, location, action hierarchy, crowd limits, weather/time, composition, theological guardrails, exclusions.

## Miracle scene
Required: scriptural event, human participants, visible environmental response, source of action remaining beyond human control, scale, reactions, lighting, prohibited magical interpretation.

## Location
Required: place identity, period, geography, terrain, vegetation, architecture, population/activity, weather/time, landmark policy, environmental composition, anachronism exclusions.

## Artifact or equipment
Required: object identity, materials, dimensions or scale, construction, wear, owner/context, display angle, background, text policy, duplicated-part exclusions.

## Symbolic or doctrine card
Required: approved theme, scriptural basis, symbol meanings, intended and unintended interpretations, composition, text separation policy, theological review status.

## Group or relationship scene
Required: lead figure, supporting figures, relationship, gaze/gesture map, spacing, hierarchy, distinct faces, interaction, environment, ambiguous-limb exclusions.

## Story or widescreen scene
Required: narrative beat, location, participants, action, cinematic direction, widescreen crop, alternate card-safe crop, continuity requirements, environmental storytelling.

## Template output skeleton

```text
[ASSET TYPE AND SUBJECT]
[NARRATIVE IDENTITY]
[VISIBLE CHARACTER OR OBJECT DETAILS]
[ACTION, EXPRESSION, AND RELATIONSHIPS]
[APPROVED SYMBOLS]
[ENVIRONMENT]
[CAMERA AND COMPOSITION]
[LIGHTING AND COLOUR]
[RENDERING AND MATERIALS]
[QUALITY AND PRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS]
[OUTPUT FORMAT]
[EXCLUSIONS]
```

## Template selection rule
Choose the narrowest template that matches the asset. A scene featuring a character is not automatically a character portrait; use the event, miracle, relationship, or story template when the action or setting is the primary narrative carrier.
