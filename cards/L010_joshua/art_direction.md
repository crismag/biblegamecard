# Art Direction

This source specification applies the shared [Visual Language](../../knowledge/art/VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md), [Character Design Guide](../../knowledge/art/CHARACTER_DESIGN_GUIDE.md), [Composition Guide](../../knowledge/art/COMPOSITION_GUIDE.md), and [Negative Prompt Library](../../knowledge/art/NEGATIVE_PROMPT_LIBRARY.md).

## Subject
- **Life stage:** mature adult commander; exact age unstated.
- **Build:** sturdy, mobile, campaign-conditioned; credible human proportions rather than exaggerated bodybuilder anatomy.
- **Face/hair:** weathered Levantine features, dark hair with modest greying, practical short-to-medium beard. These are historically plausible project choices, not scriptural facts.
- **Expression:** focused resolve and alert humility; no rage, smirk, or ecstatic magical trance.
- **Pose/action:** three-quarter figure on stable ground, torso turned toward the advance, one open hand signaling ordered movement, other hand resting near a sheathed or lowered sword.
- **Silhouette:** clear head-and-shoulders, command arm, cloak edge, and sword line at thumbnail size.

## Clothing, armour, weapon
- Layered linen/wool tunic, practical mantle, leather belt, sandals or simple footwear.
- Restrained bronze/leather defensive elements are permitted as plausible reconstruction, not asserted fact.
- Functional straight or gently curved bronze-age sword; believable grip and scabbard.
- Dust and wear communicate campaigning. Avoid royal crown, ornate fantasy pauldrons, medieval plate, Roman uniform, and pristine costume-pageant styling.

## Scene
- **Narrative mode:** synthesized pre-Jericho command portrait, not a freeze-frame claimed by Scripture.
- **Environment:** Late Bronze Age southern Levant; dry earth, limestone, sparse scrub; Jericho's fortified mass in the middle distance.
- **Supporting figures:** small, ordered Israelite procession; priests bearing the ark and ram's-horn trumpets remain secondary and respectful.
- **Weather:** clear, dry atmosphere with light dust; no supernatural storm.
- **Background:** walls intact because this is before the fall; no collapsing masonry or casualties in the reference hero image.

## Composition and camera
- Vertical 2:3 full-art illustration; three-quarter figure from slightly below eye level, not an extreme heroic low angle.
- Joshua occupies the central-left focal region; his gesture leads toward the procession and city.
- Quiet upper zone and simplified lower margins preserve title/stat overlays; no embedded typography.
- Depth order: Joshua → procession/ark → Jericho. Keep background faces non-prominent.

## Lighting and palette
- Warm directional dawn light from the side, representing readiness without equating sunlight with God.
- Controlled high contrast on face and signaling hand; atmospheric separation behind silhouette.
- Primary palette: sun-baked ochre, limestone, muted indigo, weathered leather brown.
- Accents: restrained bronze and dusty red-brown. Avoid neon gold, electric blue magic, oversaturated orange/teal grading, and radiant halo effects.

## Rendering and quality
Premium painterly biblical realism; tactile cloth, leather, bronze, stone, and dust; natural skin; print-aware detail hierarchy. Prioritize face, hands, sword contact, ark carrying poles, coherent procession scale, readable silhouette, and clean edges at card size.

## Negative considerations
Reject extra fingers/limbs, fused hands, malformed weapons, impossible scabbards, floating objects, duplicated priests, incoherent ark poles, illegible text, watermark/signature, fantasy armour, medieval/Roman equipment, modern items, gore, ethnic caricature, spellcasting, personified divine light, opened/weaponized ark, and walls falling in this pre-fall scene.
