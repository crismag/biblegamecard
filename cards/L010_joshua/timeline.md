# Timeline

Absolute dates are intentionally omitted: the repository does not resolve disputed chronologies. Order follows the canonical narrative.

| Order | Event | Reference | Significance |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leads the battle against Amalek | Exodus 17:8–16 | Earliest named command; victory remains dependent on the LORD. |
| 2 | Assists Moses at Sinai | Exodus 24:13; 32:17–18 | Establishes long service beside Moses. |
| 3 | Remains near the tent of meeting | Exodus 33:11 | Shows his assistant role; the text does not say he enters Moses' unique role. |
| 4 | Objects to Eldad and Medad prophesying | Numbers 11:24–29 | Displays loyalty and a lesson in leadership under Moses. |
| 5 | Scouts Canaan; stands with Caleb | Numbers 13:1–14:38 | Defining act of trust and minority faithfulness. |
| 6 | Commissioned before Eleazar and Israel | Numbers 27:18–23 | Public transfer of responsibility under God's direction. |
| 7 | Charged by Moses and the LORD | Deuteronomy 31:1–23 | Succession and courage are explicitly grounded in divine presence. |
| 8 | Assumes leadership after Moses' death | Deuteronomy 34:9; Joshua 1:1–18 | Begins the land-entry commission. |
| 9 | Sends spies; Rahab protects them | Joshua 2 | Prepares for Jericho and establishes the oath to Rahab. |
| 10 | Leads Israel across Jordan; sets memorial stones | Joshua 3–4 | Covenant passage and intergenerational remembrance. |
| 11 | Renews covenant signs at Gilgal; observes Passover | Joshua 5:1–12 | Frames entry by covenant obedience. |
| 12 | Encounters commander of the LORD's army | Joshua 5:13–15 | Joshua is subordinate, not depicted as the divine commander. |
| 13 | Jericho falls; Rahab is spared | Joshua 6 | Central victory through obedience to the LORD's instruction. |
| 14 | Achan's sin, defeat, judgment, then Ai | Joshua 7–8:29 | Victory is not automatic; covenant breach has communal consequence. |
| 15 | Altar and law reading at Ebal | Joshua 8:30–35 | Makes covenant instruction central to leadership. |
| 16 | Treaty with Gibeon; southern campaign | Joshua 9–10 | Includes leadership error, oath obligation, and military deliverance. |
| 17 | Northern campaign and conquest summary | Joshua 11–12 | Consolidates land-entry leadership while later text notes remaining land. |
| 18 | Oversees allotments and receives Timnath-serah | Joshua 13–21; 19:49–51 | Stewardship of inheritance rather than personal empire. |
| 19 | Resolves eastern-tribes altar crisis | Joshua 22 | Shows covenant concern and conflict de-escalation through inquiry. |
| 20 | Farewell and covenant renewal at Shechem | Joshua 23–24:28 | Calls Israel to exclusive service of the LORD. |
| 21 | Dies at 110; buried at Timnath-serah | Joshua 24:29–31 | Closes his service and records influence on the elders. |
