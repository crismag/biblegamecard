# Symbolism

| Motif | Meaning | Use rule | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sheathed or lowered sword | Readiness and command under authority | May define silhouette; avoid blood, trophies, or magical glow. | Joshua 5:13; 8:18,26 |
| Ram's-horn trumpets | Obedient procession and announced action at Jericho | Keep with priests or as grounded background detail; do not make Joshua the trumpet source unless intentionally non-literal and reviewed. | Joshua 6:4–20 |
| Ark of the covenant | The LORD's covenant presence with Israel | Background, carried by priests; never carried by Joshua, weaponized, opened, or used as a magical battery. | Joshua 3–4; 6 |
| Jordan water | Threshold, passage, and remembrance | Use for a Jordan scene, not simultaneously with Jericho in a literal scene. | Joshua 3–4 |
| Twelve stones | Memorial and teaching future generations | Exactly twelve only when representing the Jordan memorial; grounded stones, not runes. | Joshua 4:1–24 |
| Jericho wall | Formidable barrier given into Israel's hand | Architecture should read as ancient and distant; no suffering bodies or modern siege effects. | Joshua 6 |
| Land/horizon | Promised inheritance and responsibility | Warm highlands and open route may support the identity portrait without mapping disputed borders. | Joshua 1:2–6; 13–21 |
| Covenant scroll/stone witness | Law, choice, and renewal | Best for covenant-renewal variants, not clutter in the Jericho hero image. | Joshua 8:30–35; 24:25–27 |
| Banner | Unity/command | **Optional project symbol only**, since Scripture does not assign Joshua a personal banner. No sacred text or invented tribal emblem. | Project interpretation |

## Prohibited or review-required imagery
- Halo, cross, angel wings, glowing eyes, spell effects, or divine embodiment attached to Joshua.
- The commander of the LORD's army as a Joshua duplicate or casually identified figure.
- Medieval plate armour, Roman legionary equipment, firearms, modern flags, readable pseudo-Hebrew, or invented sacred sigils.
- Simultaneous literal collage of Jordan, Jericho, Ebal, and Shechem.
