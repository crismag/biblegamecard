# Joshua Failure Modes and Corrections

The full reusable matrix is in the [Artwork Review Standard](../../docs/ARTWORK_REVIEW_STANDARD.md). This table records Joshua-specific application.

| Symptoms | Likely cause | Prompt/source correction | Review recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Joshua appears too young, fashion-model smooth, or extremely elderly | “Mature” ignored or underspecified | In canonical `art.yaml` continuity fields, retain modest greying, weathering, mobile posture, and natural skin; avoid numeric age. Recompile character clause. | Reject identity drift; theology need not repeat unless life-stage meaning changes. |
| Generic European fantasy warrior or racial caricature | Genre prior or careless ethnicity shorthand | Keep geographic/material language concrete; remove “epic warrior”; prohibit stereotype traits and fantasy armour. | Historical and dignity reviewers must sign off. |
| Medieval plate, Roman lorica/helmet, modern tactical gear | “Commander” overpowers period clauses | Move linen/wool/leather/restrained bronze earlier; adapter repeats explicit period exclusions. | Reject major wardrobe error rather than retouch. |
| Two swords, floating scabbard, impossible grip | Sword mentioned without enough object constraint or generation defect | Specify a **single** functional sword, exact sheathed/lowered state, contact, grip, belt/scabbard relationship. | Inspect at 200%; local repair is a new artwork version. |
| Six fingers, missing thumb, fused command hand | Complex open-hand gesture/model failure | Simplify and enlarge open signaling hand; require five natural digits and coherent wrist. | Blocking anatomy failure until clean at print and 200%. |
| Ark floats, is opened, glows, lacks poles, or is carried by Joshua | Model sacred-object prior; ownership unclear | Keep “priests carry the closed ark on coherent poles”; exclude Joshua carrying/weaponizing it and supernatural glow. | Automatic theology rejection and source/adaptor audit. |
| Joshua emits light, has halo/glowing eyes, or casts at walls | “Divine commission,” “epic,” or victory rendered as magic | Use practical dawn light; retain divine-agency exclusion; remove abstract sacred style terms. | Automatic theology rejection. |
| Walls collapse or casualties appear | Model associates Jericho only with destruction | Move “pre-fall” and “intact walls” earlier; exclude debris, falling masonry, casualties, gore. | Reject narrative mismatch; do not crop around it. |
| Face is cross-eyed, dark, tiny, or unreadable | Too much environment; weak focal/lighting priority | Increase face scale; reaffirm face/hand as first focal points and background value separation. | Test frame, grayscale, and thumbnail after full-size anatomy review. |
| Plastic skin, crunchy edges, oversharpened dust | Adapter/upscaler bias | Request natural texture and restrained microcontrast; reduce sharpening/upscale settings and log them. | Technical/print reviewer compares raw and transformed versions. |
| Procession clones, broken poles, wrong depth/scale | Too many figures or ambiguous planes | Reduce background count; state Joshua → procession/ark → Jericho depth order and coherent occlusion/scale. | Perspective and object audit at 100–200%. |
| Poster crop fails card frame | Overlay constraints not honored | Increase quiet upper/lower regions; shift Joshua central-left without cropping head/hand/sword. | Official-frame overlay and thumbnail are mandatory before approval. |
| Looks like Moses or Caleb / generic AI hero | Generic command pose and insufficient identity cues | Preserve Joshua's ordered advance, open signal, Jericho/procession context, restrained authority, and inheritance/formation emphasis. | Collector set-sheet comparison after hard gates pass. |
