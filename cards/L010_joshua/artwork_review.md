# Joshua Artwork Review

Use the shared [Artwork Review and Evaluation Standard](../../docs/ARTWORK_REVIEW_STANDARD.md) and create one copy of [`artwork_evaluation.yaml`](../../templates/card_package/artwork_evaluation.yaml) per candidate. No candidate currently exists; these are acceptance criteria, not completed checks.

## Joshua-specific hard gates
- **Biblical/theological:** Joshua commands under the LORD's authority; he does not cast, emit, or control divine power. The ark is closed, carried by priests on poles, and never a weapon. Jericho remains intact in this pre-fall scene. No casualties or conquest spectacle.
- **Historical:** clothing, leather/bronze elements, sword, dry terrain, and architecture must be cautiously plausible for the Late Bronze Age southern Levant. No fantasy, medieval, Roman, or modern equipment.
- **Continuity:** compare face, hair, beard, build, muted-indigo/ochre palette, command gesture, single sword, and dawn-light family to canonical `art.yaml` continuity fields.
- **Visual:** Joshua's face and open signaling hand are the first focal points; procession and ark are subordinate; Jericho is third. The silhouette must survive the official frame and thumbnail.
- **Technical/print:** require exact candidate hash and generation record; inspect full resolution, 200%, official-frame overlay, grayscale, and thumbnail. Embedded text or watermark is blocking.
- **Collector/set:** Joshua must read as an ordered covenant commander, not a generic berserker or Moses/Caleb duplicate.

## Candidate decision
Approval requires the shared weighted score of at least 90%, every criterion at least 3/5, no critical defect, and named approvals from biblical/theological, art, and technical reviewers. Historical, print, and collector findings must be resolved or explicitly accepted by the responsible gate approver. Retouching creates a new immutable artwork version and repeats affected reviews.
