# Canonical Identity

## Scripture

| Field | Canonical record |
|---|---|
| Full name | Joshua son of Nun |
| Alternative name | Hoshea son of Nun; Moses called him Joshua |
| Hebrew name | יְהוֹשֻׁעַ (*Yehoshua*); הוֹשֵׁעַ (*Hoshea*) in Numbers 13:8,16 |
| Name meaning | Common lexical understanding: “YHWH is salvation” / “the LORD saves” for Joshua; “salvation” for Hoshea. This is linguistic explanation, not an explicit narrative gloss. |
| Titles/descriptions | Moses' assistant/servant; leader commissioned to succeed Moses; servant of the LORD at his death |
| Roles | Spy representing Ephraim; military leader; Moses' assistant; successor; covenant leader |
| Occupation | Scripture describes offices and actions rather than a modern occupation. |
| Nation/tribe | Israel; tribe of Ephraim |
| Historical period | Wilderness generation and Israel's entry into Canaan, before the judges |
| Primary locations | Wilderness/Sinai region, Rephidim, Kadesh area, plains of Moab, Jordan, Gilgal, Jericho, Ai, Mount Ebal, Gibeon, Canaan, Shiloh, Shechem, Timnath-serah |
| Calling | Lead Israel across the Jordan and apportion the land; be strong and courageous; keep the law |
| Predecessor | Moses |
| Named successor | None. Joshua 24 closes with Joshua's death and the elders who outlived him. |

### Major life events
- Commands Israel against Amalek (Exodus 17:8–16).
- Accompanies Moses and serves near the tent (Exodus 24:13; 33:11).
- Scouts Canaan and, with Caleb, urges trust in the LORD (Numbers 13–14).
- Is publicly commissioned as Moses' successor (Numbers 27:18–23; Deuteronomy 31:1–8,14–23; 34:9).
- Leads the Jordan crossing, covenant signs, campaigns, and land allotment (Joshua 1–22).
- Leads covenant renewal at Shechem (Joshua 23–24).
- Dies at 110 and is buried at Timnath-serah (Joshua 24:29–30; Judges 2:8–9).

## Traditional understanding
- English tradition commonly renders יְהוֹשֻׁעַ as Joshua and understands the name as “the LORD saves.”
- Christian interpretation often notes the related Hebrew/Greek naming of Joshua and Jesus. This package does not turn that theological observation into visual equivalence or claim Joshua is Jesus.
- Exact absolute dates, facial features, armour design, and Joshua's age during the conquest are not supplied by Scripture.

## Project interpretation
- **Card title:** “The Conqueror” is a product title, not a biblical quotation.
- **Identity emphasis:** courageous, obedient commander whose victories depend upon God's presence and Israel's covenant faithfulness—not autonomous military power.
- **Visual life stage:** mature leader is a continuity choice. It must not be labeled as a known age.
- **Hero scene:** command before Jericho is a synthesized identity portrait, not a claim that Scripture describes this precise pose, costume, weather, or camera view.
