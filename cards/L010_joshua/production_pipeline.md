# Joshua Production Pipeline

This package instantiates the reusable [Production Validation Standard](../../docs/PRODUCTION_VALIDATION_STANDARD.md). A stage marked `blocked` has not failed; it cannot begin until its upstream gate passes.

| Stage | Joshua-specific purpose | Inputs | Outputs/artifacts | Dependencies | Required review | Blocking failure conditions | State |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Canonical knowledge | Establish Joshua as commissioned successor, covenant leader, and land-entry commander. | `identity.md`, `biblical_basis.md`, `timeline.md`, `relationships.md`, `legacy.md` v1.0.0 | Reviewed canonical content v1.0.0 | Registry L010; theology guide; cited passages | Biblical/theological reviewer; G1 | Unclassified claim, missing passage, conquest framing concern, Joshua credited with divine power | `review_requested` |
| 2. Gameplay identity | Express coordinated command, fortification pressure, and inheritance without balanced rules. | Canonical content v1.0.0; `gameplay_identity.md`; gameplay guide | canonical `data/gameplay.yaml` v1.0.0 | G1; registry archetype | Gameplay + theology reviewers; G2 | Moses/Caleb overlap, cruelty incentive, ark weaponization, personal miracle mechanic | `review_requested` |
| 3. Art direction | Define a sober pre-Jericho portrait and stable Joshua continuity. | Knowledge v1.0.0; `symbolism.md`; gameplay emphasis | canonical `data/art.yaml` v1.0.0 and `art_direction.md` review projection | G1–G2; shared art standards | Historical, theology, art reviewers; G3 | Unresolved life stage, anachronism, collapsing-wall spectacle, continuity ambiguity | `review_requested` |
| 4. | 11. Official release | Archive and publish a locked Joshua bundle. | All approvals, dependency lock, checksums, release notes | Release bundle, checksums, registry/tracker state | G7 | Production approver | Open critical issue, incomplete provenance, stale dependency, missing rights record | `blocked` |

## Current stopping point
The compilation request is reproducible from repository inputs, but generation must not begin until reviewers approve G1–G4 and a concrete model adapter, model revision, resolution, and exposed settings are recorded in a new manifest revision. The neutral adapter is deliberately not sufficient for production generation.
